About Us

Our Story

Building Brands Since 1991

M/S Kanya International is a Proprietary Firm of Mr. Purshotam L. Sajnani, working in Pune since 1991. What started as a focused newspaper advertising agency has grown into a full-service creative powerhouse serving clients across India.

M/s Kanya International works as an Advertising Agency for Classified and Display Advertising in Newspapers all over India. We specialize in Newspaper Advertisements, Printing Services, Logo & Artwork Design, and Website Design — providing end-to-end creative, print, and web solutions to help brands grow.

M/s Kanya International has its own dedicated staff and team to work together on all projects taken by the Company. We are committed to working at our best to satisfy and provide better services to our Clients and Advertisers.
